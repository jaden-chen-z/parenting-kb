## 每月语言里程碑

- **咿呀阶段**:"coos, gurgles, and screeches of earlier months now giving way to recognizable syllables, such as 'ba,' 'da,' 'ga,' and 'ma'"
- **意外说词**:婴儿可能意外说出 "mama" 和 "bye-bye"
- **有意使用**:当成人表示兴奋时,婴儿意识到他们说了有意义的东西

## 第一个有意义的词

- 有的婴儿在满一岁时掌握 2-3 个词汇
- 更常见的是 12 个月时仍说 "gibberish that has the tones and variations of intelligible speech"
- 词汇定义:任何 "consistently refers to the same person, object, or event" 的声音

## 理解词汇 vs 表达词汇

- **理解能力**:婴儿理解的语言 "more than you suspect"
- **表达能力**:较理解能力明显滞后
- 示例:提及最爱玩具时会看向它,说明理解概念

## 手势发展

- 婴儿通过 "pointing, crawling, or gesturing toward her target" 进行非语言交流
- "imitate many of the gestures she sees adults make as they talk"

## AAP 操作建议

**对话策略**:
- "Tell her what's happening around her, particularly as you bathe, change, and feed her"
- 使用简单且具体的语言

**命名物体**:
- "Verbally label familiar toys and objects for her, and try to be as consistent as possible"
- 保持一致的命名(如不要时而叫 "cat" 时而叫 "kitty")

**阅读**:
- 选择带 "large board, cloth, or vinyl pages that she can turn herself" 的图书
- 寻找 "simple but colorful illustrations of things your child will recognize"

**互动**:
- "Ask questions and wait for a response"
- "If she says 'Gaagaagaa,' repeat it back"
- 这向婴儿表明 "communication is two-way"
