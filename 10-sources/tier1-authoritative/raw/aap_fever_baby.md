# Fever and Your Baby

**Source URL**: https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/fever/Pages/Fever-and-Your-Baby.aspx
**Fetched**: 2026-05-02
**Org**: American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) · HealthyChildren.org

---

## 正常体温与发烧定义
- "A rectal temperature reading of 100.4 degrees Fahrenheit (38 degrees Celsius) or higher is considered a fever"
- 婴幼儿体温通常高于年长儿童
- 下午和傍晚体温最高,午夜和清晨最低

## 不同月龄的应对指南
- **3 个月以下**:直肠温度 ≥ 100.4°F (38°C),立即拨打儿科医生
- **3-6 个月**:温度 ≥ 101°F (38.3°C) 通知医生
- **6 个月以上**:温度 ≥ 103°F (39.4°C) 通知医生

## 立即就医的红旗信号
- 3 个月或更小的婴儿出现任何发烧:"even if your baby shows no other signs or symptoms of being ill"
- 呼吸困难
- 高热惊厥超过 15 分钟

## 测温方法精度
- 直肠测温:"most reliable and accurate for infants, especially babies under 3 months old"
- 避免在 6 个月以下婴儿使用耳温计(耳道过小)
- 不可靠方法:触摸额头、温度敏感贴纸
