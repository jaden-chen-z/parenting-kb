# Teething Necklaces and Beads: A Caution for Parents

**Source URL**: https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/teething-tooth-care/Pages/Amber-Teething-Necklaces.aspx
**Fetched**: 2026-05-02
**Org**: American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) · HealthyChildren.org

---

When parents observe their baby experiencing discomfort, they naturally seek solutions. Teething necklaces and beads have grown increasingly popular as an alternative approach to address teething pain. However, the evidence is clear: they are neither effective nor safe.

## FDA Warning and Safety Concerns

The Food and Drug Administration issued an official warning in December 2018 following reports of children choking on broken beads and a tragic incident involving an 18-month-old who died from strangulation by an amber necklace during sleep.

## Product Claims Lack Scientific Support

These necklaces, made from amber, wood, marble, or silicone, are marketed to relieve teething discomfort and sometimes promote sensory stimulation. Retailers claim that body heat causes amber to release pain-relieving substances absorbed through skin. Some promoters assert these necklaces stimulate the thyroid gland to control drooling and enhance immune function to reduce inflammation.

According to integrative medicine expert Dr. Andrew Weil, "the use of these necklaces is not supported by modern science." No scientific research validates these marketing claims.

## Documented Hazards

The risks are twofold: strangulation and choking. The American Academy of Pediatrics emphasizes that suffocation represents the leading cause of death for infants under one year and ranks among the top five causes for children ages 1-4.

## Safe Alternatives

Proven methods to soothe teething discomfort include:

- Plastic and rubber chew toys
- Damp, frozen washcloths (not solid-frozen teething rings)
- Gentle gum massage with clean hands
- Acetaminophen under pediatrician guidance
- Avoiding benzocaine-containing numbing gels

The AAP does not recommend infants wear any jewelry.
