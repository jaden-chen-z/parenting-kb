## 8 Months
- Beginning to become more mobile
- May sit without support, though occasionally topples
- Uses arms to catch herself when falling
- Trunk muscles strengthening
- Starts leaning to reach objects

## 9 Months
- Should be sitting by herself independently
- Constant motion when on flat surfaces
- Arches neck to look around when on stomach
- Grabs feet or nearby objects and pulls to mouth
- Turns over at will, flips without notice
- Crawling typically mastered "between seven and ten months"

## 10 Months
- Actively crawling, scooting, or slithering
- May rock on hands and knees
- Some babies push backward initially
- Propels forward across room toward objects
- Alternative locomotion methods acceptable if coordinated

## 11 Months
- Pulls to standing position frequently
- May not know how to get down initially
- Attempts tentative steps while holding support
- Cruises alongside furniture
- Occasionally lets go before seeking support again

## 12 Months
- "Many babies' first steps are taken around their first birthday"
- Walks with feet wide apart for balance
- May move too fast and fall when stopping
- Learning to stop and change directions
- Can squat, pick up objects, and stand again

## Red Flags (AAP Alerts)
- "If she is not sitting by herself by nine months, let your pediatrician know"
- Baby not moving normally or coordinating both body sides equally
- Concerns about motor development warrant pediatrician discussion

## Safety & Equipment Recommendations
- Retire changing table; use floor or bed to prevent falls
- "Never leave her alone for an instant at any time"
- Remove small objects (buttons, coin, balloon fragments) from floor
- Install "sturdy gates at the top and bottom of your staircase"
- Teach backward stair descent
- **Avoid baby walkers**: "The American Academy of Pediatrics strongly urges parents not to use baby walkers"
- Consider stationary exercise saucer or sturdy push toys instead

## Footwear Guidance
- Choose "closed-toe, comfortable, and flexible" shoes with nonskid soles
- Sneakers recommended
- "Your child does not need wedges, inserts, high backs, reinforced heels, special arches"
- Check fit monthly; first pair lasts 2-3 months
