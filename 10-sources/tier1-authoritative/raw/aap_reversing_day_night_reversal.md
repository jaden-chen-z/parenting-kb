Some newborns develop a "day-night reversal" sleep pattern where they sleep more during daytime hours and stay awake demanding feeding and entertainment throughout the night. While exhausting for parents, this pattern typically resolves as babies learn to consolidate sleep into longer stretches.

## Lights On, Lights Off

To establish healthier sleep habits early, create clear environmental distinctions between day and night:

**During the day:**
- "Allow for active sleep. During the day, don't worry if your baby falls asleep in more 'active' areas of the house - in rooms with light or music on, for example."
- Permit background household noises and normal daytime activities

**During the night:**
- Keep interactions "calm and quiet" with minimal background noise
- "Maintain focus. Whenever possible, take a more focused approach to your nighttime interactions—limiting them to feeding, burping, changing, and gentle soothing when necessary."
- Use dark rooms and soft voice when signaling sleep time

The article emphasizes this approach helps establish "more acceptable sleep habits in the future," though results require patience rather than overnight transformation.
