# Fever: When to Call the Pediatrician

**Source URL**: https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/fever/Pages/When-to-Call-the-Pediatrician.aspx
**Fetched**: 2026-05-02
**Org**: American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) · HealthyChildren.org

---

## 0-3 个月发烧红旗
- "Is younger than 3 months (12 weeks) and has a temperature of 100.4°F (38.0°C) or higher"

## 行为红旗
- "Looks very ill, is unusually drowsy, or is very fussy"
- "Your child still 'acts sick' once their fever is brought down"

## 呼吸红旗
- "breathing difficulty"

## 其他立即就医症状
- "stiff neck, severe headache, severe sore throat, severe ear pain"
- "unexplained rash, or repeated vomiting or diarrhea"
- "Has had a seizure"
- "Temperature rises above 104°F (40°C) repeatedly for a child of any age"

## 持续发烧时间提醒
- < 2 岁:超过 24 小时需就医
- ≥ 2 岁:超过 72 小时需就医
